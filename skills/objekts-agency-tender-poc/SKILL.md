# objekts Agency Tender and PoC Production

Use this skill when an agency, production company or creative team needs to establish whether a visual idea is producible before committing to the full job.

Do not overproduce a pitch. Identify the uncertainty whose proof changes confidence in the whole project. Good PoC targets include exact product fidelity under a difficult camera move, recurring character continuity, AI/CG integration, environment transformation, a vertical recomposition, a difficult transition or morph, or a specific motion language. State what the test proves and what result counts as pass or fail.

Choose the smallest useful deliverable ladder: visual research, treatment support, lookdev, feasibility note, hero-shot PoC, motion test, rough animatic or preliminary production design. Preserve hard locks and clearly distinguish experimental material from approved production assets. The goal is to reduce expensive uncertainty and give the agency a credible production route without building the entire film inside the tender.
