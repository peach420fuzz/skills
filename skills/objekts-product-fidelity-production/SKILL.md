# objekts Product Fidelity Production

Use this skill for product films, FMCG, bottles, packaging, automotive, devices, fashion objects, packshots, or any visual-production job where the hero object must remain exact.

Separate the approved source-of-truth domains before choosing a generation route. Determine which reference controls product geometry, packaging and label layout, logo and typography, material response, reflections, color and scale. An approved packshot can still be insufficient for production if it contains only one usable angle or cannot survive the intended camera move. Mark such material as fit-with-rebuild rather than silently inventing missing geometry through generation.

Keep exact brand graphics and legal typography deterministic. Use controlled CGI, source assets or compositing where geometry and materials must remain stable. When the same product repeats across shots, prove representative shots first and compare them side-by-side for geometry, packaging and material drift.

This is a first-party public skill from **objekts** and links to the same methodology used by objekts Production Desk.
